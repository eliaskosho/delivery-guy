How it works

Three stops. One switch.

  1. 01

    Someone trades $DELIVERY

    Every buy and every sell on pump.fun carries a 0.3% fee. Volume is the engine.

  2. 02

    The switch was thrown at launch

    pump.fun lets a creator keep that fee or hand it to traders as cashback. It is chosen once, before launch, and can never be flipped back. We pointed it at the traders.

  3. 03

    It goes back to the traders

    Cashback is earned by the wallets doing the trading. Buy once and sit still and you earn nothing from it. This is not a hold-to-earn token.

Trader Cashback is pump.fun's own mechanism. It is run by pump.fun, not by this site and not by a contract we control. The schedule it pays on, and where you see or claim it, is theirs — check your position on pump.fun.
